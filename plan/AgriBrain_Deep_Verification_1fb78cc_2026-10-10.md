## 4. Remaining reproduced boundaries

### RV01 — Global cutoff still depends on successful ledger access

**Priority: P0.** Related target: F16–F19; R3 AB-EXE-004, AB-ARC-006.

Source: [dispatcher.py:1022–1083](https://github.com/webClone/AgriWise/blob/1fb78cc53f4327cf1e6dc641d6db368309f7c793/services/agribrain/irrigation_runtime/dispatcher.py#L1022-L1083). In the `except` block, line 1074 calls `SharedQuotaStore.update_status()` without shielding that second failure. Registry fallback is after the loop, rather than protected by a surrounding `finally`.

**Reproduction.** Admit running A and B. Make A's emergency stop raise a transport error. With a healthy ledger, B receives a STOP and fallback runs. With malformed ledger JSON, or with `_write_state()` raising `OSError`, the handler throws before B is reached.

| Condition | B STOP calls | B simulated running | Registry fallback | Method outcome |
|---|---:|---|---|---|
| A transport exception; healthy ledger | 1 | False | Called | Complete result dictionary |
| A transport exception; corrupt ledger | 0 | True | Skipped | `CorruptedQuotaStoreError` |
| A transport exception; ledger write failure | 0 | True | Skipped | `OSError` |

**Required repair.** Execute every cutoff attempt independently of accounting success. Separate controller results from persistence diagnostics; protect each independently, retain local unresolved ownership, and attempt registry fallback in a `finally` that also survives persistence errors. Continue to block new admissions if the ledger is unhealthy. Persist unresolved stop intent when storage recovers without manufacturing stop confirmation.

**Acceptance.** Repeat both combined faults with at least two actuators; every event receives a cutoff attempt and a result entry; fallback runs; accounting failure is exposed without preventing later actuator cutoff. This demonstrates software loop behavior, not field cutoff latency.

Evidence: `recovery-boundary-results.json → emergency_storage_boundary`.

### RV02 — Scientific history length is incorrectly used as publication generation

**Priority: P1.** Related target: F08/F09; R3 AB-DAT-012, AB-ARC-007/008.

Source: [runner.py:1218–1248](https://github.com/webClone/AgriWise/blob/1fb78cc53f4327cf1e6dc641d6db368309f7c793/services/agribrain/layer3_decision/runner.py#L1218-L1248), [payload sequence at 2461](https://github.com/webClone/AgriWise/blob/1fb78cc53f4327cf1e6dc641d6db368309f7c793/services/agribrain/layer3_decision/runner.py#L2461), and [canonical publication sequence](https://github.com/webClone/AgriWise/blob/1fb78cc53f4327cf1e6dc641d6db368309f7c793/services/agribrain/world_model/canonical_worldline.py#L332-L355).

The runner chooses `payload.sequence` ahead of the checkpoint record's sequence. The former is `len(merged_history)`; the latter increases on every committed publication. A corrected historical trajectory can legitimately shorten the history and move the current scientific step backward while advancing the publication generation.

**Reproduction uses two actual OS processes.** Parent commits dry Jan 2 and Jan 3: publication sequence 2, payload sequence 2. A separate process revises Jan 2 rainfall to 40 mm and publishes a new head: publication sequence 3, payload sequence 1. Parent then asks for Jan 3 again.

| Reader | Jan 3 top-layer VWC | Durable publication after query | RAM/disk head relationship |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Warm parent | 0.1730 | 3; still corrected Jan 2 head | Different; old Jan 3 RAM head retained |
| Cold parent control | 0.2124 | 4; corrected Jan 3 propagation | Matching |

The difference is **0.0394 m³/m³**. The warm worker returns the obsolete profile and does not advance from the corrected predecessor. This is an invalidation and result-identity failure, not just an outdated diagnostic counter.

**Required repair.** Use the monotonically increasing committed record/head generation for publication fencing. Keep transition count and model date separate. When disk generation is newer, invalidate and hydrate the affected trajectory even when the corrected scientific history is shorter. Update generation tokens consistently in cold hydration, normal publication, replay, and rollback. Under the scope lock, revalidate a captured disk token before treating it as current.

**Acceptance.** Repeat two-process historical revisions that shorten, preserve, and extend history. Warm/cold next-day profiles and checkpoint identities must agree; no stale RAM result may survive a newer disk publication. Also retain the original historical-reader and three-worker rollback controls.

Evidence: `publication-generation-results.json → foreign_historical_correction`; passing original race controls are in the two hydraulic boundary result files.

### RV03 — Ledger-derived recovery grants an expired operation a new deadline

**Priority: P1.** Related target: F16/F18; R3 AB-EXE-003/004, AB-ARC-008.

Source: [fresh active reconstruction](https://github.com/webClone/AgriWise/blob/1fb78cc53f4327cf1e6dc641d6db368309f7c793/services/agribrain/irrigation_runtime/dispatcher.py#L220-L273), [restart elapsed calculation](https://github.com/webClone/AgriWise/blob/1fb78cc53f4327cf1e6dc641d6db368309f7c793/services/agribrain/irrigation_runtime/dispatcher.py#L1115-L1134), and [reservation fields](https://github.com/webClone/AgriWise/blob/1fb78cc53f4327cf1e6dc641d6db368309f7c793/services/agribrain/irrigation_runtime/safety.py#L667-L685).

The durable reservation contains a mutable record timestamp but no authoritative first-dispatch deadline or original `actual_start`. A fresh `submit_event()` reconstructs the phase and payload but leaves `actual_start=""`. Restart initializes elapsed time to zero when that field is absent. A process-local monotonic origin cannot be reconstructed from a newly registered monitor.

**Reproduction.** An executing ten-minute event has an original start eleven minutes ago. Recovery from a copy of the original event stops it immediately. Recovery from the durable duplicate returned to a fresh caller remains `EXECUTING`: running=True, STOP calls=0, monitor elapsed=0 seconds. The original elapsed simulated time is 661 seconds. Failed-stop recovery similarly retains stop intent but resets the fresh monitor's origin.

**Qualification.** Original-owner late fencing is repaired, and recovery with a separately persisted valid start timestamp is a passing control. This finding concerns ledger-derived reconstruction; it does not claim every restart resets its deadline.

**Required repair.** Persist immutable first-send/start timing and a qualified deadline before physical dispatch. Repeated reservations/status updates must not change them. Hydrate these fields on duplicate joins and restart; distinguish pending send from acknowledged start. If elapsed time for potentially live execution cannot be established, retain uncertainty and conservatively expire/stop rather than grant a new allowance. Use monotonic time within a process and a documented restart-safe clock policy across processes.

**Acceptance.** An expired operation reconstructed only from durable records cannot obtain additional runtime. Cover pending START, successful ACK, failed late fence, query failure, and absent/corrupt timing, while preserving flow, area, phase, and reservation.

Evidence: `recovery-boundary-results.json → expired_executing_recovery_control, duplicate_restart_deadline`.

### RV05 — Restart and duration fallback still manufacture qualified observations

**Priority: P1.** Related target: F17/F18 and downstream evidence qualification; R3 AB-DAT-005/006/007/010, AB-EXE-002/005.

Source: [idle restart settlement](https://github.com/webClone/AgriWise/blob/1fb78cc53f4327cf1e6dc641d6db368309f7c793/services/agribrain/irrigation_runtime/dispatcher.py#L1265-L1267), [duration fallback](https://github.com/webClone/AgriWise/blob/1fb78cc53f4327cf1e6dc641d6db368309f7c793/services/agribrain/irrigation_runtime/dispatcher.py#L893-L901), and [store observation defaults](https://github.com/webClone/AgriWise/blob/1fb78cc53f4327cf1e6dc641d6db368309f7c793/services/agribrain/irrigation_runtime/safety.py#L900-L918).

Two integration counterexamples remain:

1. Restart sees the controller `ONLINE` and directly sets `COMPLETED`, settling the commanded 8 mm without any delivery measurement. The receipt simultaneously says actual litres=0.0, actual duration=0.0, area=1 ha, and delivered depth=8.0 mm. For a qualified observation, \(I=V/(10{,}000 A)\); the recorded zero litres would imply zero depth. Here the zeros are fabricated defaults, so neither the depth nor the zero-valued observations are qualified measurements.
2. Ordinary validation receives 40,000 L and area 1 ha but no actual duration. It correctly derives 4 mm, then writes actual duration=10 min from the commanded duration. The missing observed duration has silently become a physical outcome.

**Qualification.** Conservative target-based exposure is useful for safety accounting. It must remain distinguishable from a measured dose and cannot establish a physically safe upper bound on an overrun. Missing duration need not prohibit a separately qualified volume/depth settlement; it must remain missing or explicitly estimated.

**Required repair.** Route idle restart to unresolved delivery/validation, or retain an explicitly estimated exposure record with suitable authority. Require measured-volume support for measured depth. Preserve absent observation fields as absent, with field-level provenance. Never label target duration as actual duration without an explicit qualified estimate. Apply the same settlement contract across normal completion, restart, cancellation, and stopped-delivery correction.

**Acceptance.** Idle status alone cannot certify measured delivery. Zero measurements remain distinguishable from missing measurements. A known volume with unknown duration retains that missingness in the receipt and downstream hydraulic/training views.

Evidence: `recovery-boundary-results.json → qualified_observation_recovery`.
