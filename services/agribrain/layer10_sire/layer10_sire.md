## 4. Key Mathematical Formulations

### A. Bilinear Upscaling & Mapping
Determines high-resolution pixel values ($v$) from coarse grid coordinates:
$$\text{src\_r} = (r + 0.5) \cdot \frac{oH}{nH} - 0.5$$
$$v = v_{00}(1 - f_r)(1 - f_c) + v_{01}(1 - f_r)f_c + v_{10}f_r(1 - f_c) + v_{11}f_rf_c$$
Where $oH, nH$ are old/new heights, and $f_r, f_c$ are row/column fractional steps.

### B. High-Resolution 1m Texture Sharpening (V65G)
High-resolution sharpened values are generated by blending upscaled certified rasters with localized detail textures derived from NDVI or NDMI gradients:
$$\text{Texture} = \text{Basis}_{up} - \text{mean}_{neighbors}(\text{Basis}_{up})$$
$$\text{Sharpened} = \text{Certified}_{up} + \text{Texture} \cdot \left( \text{VisualIntensity} \cdot \frac{\sigma_{certified}}{\sigma_{texture}} \right)$$
Where the visual intensity factor defaults to $0.40$. Sharpened outputs are strictly mean-preserved to maintain physical consistency.

### C. Explainability Value at Risk (VaR)
$$\text{VaR}_{baseline} = 150 + \sum_{d \in \text{Diagnoses}} \left( \text{severity}_d \cdot \text{confidence}_d \right) \cdot 45$$
