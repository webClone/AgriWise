## 2. Technical Analysis & Current Deficiencies

### The Correlation vs Causation Prescriptive Risk
In traditional agronomic software, recommendation engines observe correlations in historical observational data:
- Example: "Plots with higher fungicide spend yielded $12\%$ more."
- In reality, farmers spray more fungicide on high-yielding, high-density fields with higher yield potential. The correlation is confounded by field management intensity.
- If a world model optimizes prescriptions based on observational correlations without a formal **causal identification strategy** (e.g. Pearl's Structural Causal Models / do-calculus), it will recommend expensive, unnecessary chemical interventions that do not cause the projected yield gain.

## 3. Implementation Plan

### Step 1: Formalize Structural Causal Models (SCM) for Interventions
In `services/agribrain/world_model/causal/scm.py`:
- Represent agronomic causal relationships as Directed Acyclic Graphs (DAGs):
  $$\text{Action } A \longrightarrow \text{State Change } \Delta S \longrightarrow \text{Yield } Y$$
  $$\text{Confounder } Z \text{ (Soil Texture, Rain, Topography)} \longrightarrow (A, \Delta S, Y)$$
- Implement interventional Pearl do-operator:
  $$P(Y \mid \text{do}(A = a)) = \sum_z P(Y \mid A = a, Z = z) P(Z = z)$$
- Clearly distinguish:
  1. **Associational Query:** $P(Y \mid A = a)$ (Observational correlation).
  2. **Interventional Query:** $P(Y \mid \text{do}(A = a))$ (Actual treatment effect).
  3. **Counterfactual Query:** $P(Y_{A=a'} \mid A = a, Y = y)$ ("What would yield have been if we had applied $30\text{ mm}$ less water?").

### Step 2: Confounder De-Biasing & Synthetic Baselines
In `services/agribrain/world_model/causal/debiasing.py`:
- Use weather-adjusted synthetic controls and propensity score weighting to isolate treatment effects from ambient rain events.
- Never label single-plot pre/post observations as causal without explicit confounder adjustment.

### Step 3: Bounded Policy Optimization
In Layer 8 and prescriptive planners:
- Restrict optimization of action dosages strictly within the **support of the qualified causal model**:
  - If no historical or trial data exists for applying $300\text{ kg N/ha}$, the optimizer is mathematically prohibited from extrapolating beyond the convex hull of evidence.
- Every counterfactual projection must state:
  - Required unconfoundedness assumptions.
  - Confidence interval bounds.
  - Worst-case downside risk if the intervention fails.
