## 2. Technical Analysis & Current Deficiencies

### The Premature Authority Risk
MESIA is an experimental sensing modality attempting to infer environmental and soil moisture variations from ambient cellular, Wi-Fi, and LoRa radio frequency attenuation.
Granting MESIA direct authority to alter ASMAE soil moisture states or trigger irrigation before rigorous, multi-season field validation presents severe risks:
- Radio multipath interference, tractor movements, and atmospheric humidity swings produce nuisance signals that mimic soil drying or wetting.
- Without a strict shadow-mode trial, uncalibrated radio innovations will corrupt the stable satellite-weather water balance.

---

## 3. Implementation Plan

### Step 1: Enforce Permanent Shadow Operation Mode
In `services/agribrain/mesia/integration.py` and `layer05_asmae/`:
- Enforce that MESIA proposals run strictly in **SHADOW MODE**:
  1. Primary state is updated solely via qualified satellite, weather, and calibrated soil capacitance probes.
  2. MESIA innovations are computed in parallel and logged to an isolated shadow worldline track (`shadow_mesia_posterior`).
  3. MESIA is prohibited from writing to canonical production worldlines or influencing Layer 8 Prescriptions.

### Step 2: Implement Residual & Drift Analytics Engine
In `services/agribrain/mesia/analytics/shadow_evaluation.py`:
- Compute daily residual statistics against physical in-situ capacitance/TDR probes:
  $$\text{RMSE} = \sqrt{\frac{1}{N} \sum_{i=1}^N (\hat{\theta}_{\text{MESIA}, i} - \theta_{\text{TDR}, i})^2}$$
  $$\text{Bias} = \frac{1}{N} \sum_{i=1}^N (\hat{\theta}_{\text{MESIA}, i} - \theta_{\text{TDR}, i})$$
- Track nuisance sensitivity: correlate MESIA innovations with wind speed, antenna vibrations, and farm machinery telemetry to ensure rejection of non-biological interference.

### Step 3: Hardcoded Emergency Kill Switch
Provide a dynamic configuration toggle in `services/agribrain/mesia/contracts/`:
```python
MESIA_GLOBAL_ENABLE = False  # Hard kill-switch: drops all MESIA packets instantly
MESIA_STATE_AUTHORITY_ENABLE = False  # Must remain False until Phase 3 Promotion Gate passes
```

## 4. Acceptance Criteria & Promotion Gate
- [ ] MESIA runs strictly in parallel shadow mode; canonical production water states are completely unaffected.
- [ ] Over a 90-day shadow trial with at least 3 rainfall and drying cycles, MESIA achieves:
  - $\text{RMSE} \le 0.04\text{ m}^3/\text{m}^3$ against physical ground-truth TDR probes.
  - Zero false-positive rain detections caused by farm machinery or multipath reflections.
- [ ] The kill-switch immediately disables radio packet processing with 0 latency penalty to the main orchestrator.
- [ ] Promotion to live state authority requires explicit written sign-off by the Agronomy Science Board.
