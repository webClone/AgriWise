## 2. Technical Analysis & Current Deficiencies

### The Uncalibrated Forward Rollout Hazard
Before an agronomical world model can be trusted to simulate counterfactual "what-if" scenarios, it must demonstrate that it can accurately forecast real forward trajectories under passive conditions:
- If a world model cannot accurately predict soil moisture or crop canopy development 7 days into the future under known weather, it cannot be trusted to evaluate alternative nitrogen or irrigation doses.
- Multi-step autoregressive rollouts often suffer from compounding error drift: small errors on Day 1 compound into absurd physical states (e.g. soil moisture $<0$ or $\text{LAI} > 20$) by Day 14.

## 3. Implementation Plan

### Step 1: Implement Physics-Constrained Dynamic Rollout Engine
In `services/agribrain/world_model/dynamics/rollout.py`:
- Propagate state across a $K$-day horizon using ensemble weather disturbances:
  $$\mathbf{s}_{t+k} = f(\mathbf{s}_{t+k-1}, \mathbf{a}_{t+k-1}, \mathbf{w}_{t+k-1}; \boldsymbol{\theta})$$
- Apply hard physical conservation projections at every step:
  - Water balance: $\sum_i \Delta S_i = \text{Inflow} - \text{Outflow}$.
  - Storage bounds: $\theta_r \le \theta_i \le \theta_{\text{sat}}$.
  - Biomass monotonicity: Living crop biomass cannot spontaneously decrease unless senescence or frost damage occurs.

### Step 2: Continuous Ranked Probability Score (CRPS) Evaluation
In `services/agribrain/world_model/evaluation/calibration.py`:
- Measure probabilistic calibration against independent future observations across 1, 3, 7, and 14-day horizons:
  $$\text{CRPS}(F, y) = \int_{-\infty}^{\infty} (F(x) - \mathbf{1}(x \ge y))^2 \, dx$$
- Compute empirical coverage probability: for a $90\%$ prediction interval, verify that the observed ground truth falls within the interval between $85\%$ and $95\%$ of the time.

### Step 3: Latent State Unidentifiability Reporting
If incoming observations cannot mathematically distinguish between two latent explanations (e.g. surface drying vs deep root uptake, or nitrogen deficiency vs sulfur deficiency):
- Report both hypotheses in the world model state with explicit probability distributions, rather than arbitrarily picking one.

## 4. Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] Multi-step forward rollouts maintain zero mass/energy balance violations over 30-day forecast horizons.
- [ ] 7-day soil moisture depletion forecasts achieve lower CRPS than climatological persistence baselines across Tier 1 crops.
- [ ] Empirical uncertainty coverage is calibrated ($90\%$ prediction intervals contain $\ge 85\%$ of observations).
- [ ] Identifiability limits are documented; unresolvable physical ambiguities are reported as multi-modal posteriors.
